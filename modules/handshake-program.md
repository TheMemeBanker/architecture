# handshake program

an open call inviting devs, dreamers, and innovators to propose and build with rig — requires a small arc deposit to prevent spam.

## how it connects

- accepted handshake projects can launch through [arc forge](arc-forge.md)
- contributors earn through the [$arc token](arc-token.md) feedback & rewards loop
- proposals feed the [continuous innovation loop](continuous-innovation-loop.md)

ready to dive deeper? join the handshake program to collaborate, build AI use cases, or explore more about the ecosystem.
