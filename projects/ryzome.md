## how ryzome works

create powerful AI workflows that solve real problems in minutes, not days.

1. **start with your knowledge** — import your existing notes or start fresh. every idea becomes a node on your infinite canvas. write in markdown, add images, organize visually.
2. **connect your thoughts** — draw connections between related ideas. each link preserves context, building a knowledge graph that mirrors how you actually think.
3. **amplify with AI** — every node is ai-powered. generate new insights, expand concepts, or synthesize connected ideas. the AI understands your entire context chain.

this isn't software. it's a new way of thinking — where AI agents don't just respond, they collaborate, create, and surprise you with possibilities you hadn't imagined.
