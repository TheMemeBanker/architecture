# arc forge

a streamlined token launch platform built atop meteora's dynamic liquidity market maker (dlmm) and integrated with jupiter routing. through arc forge, handshake projects (for now) can seamlessly issue and manage their tokens with distinct advantages such as deeper liquidity, reliable price stability, and built-in defenses against sniper bots — all while routing liquidity flows through the arc token.

## key advantages (6)

### deeper, more efficient liquidity

meteora's dlmm organizes liquidity into discrete price bins, enabling high capital efficiency. with targeted distribution, projects achieve robust liquidity while minimizing slippage and price impact within active bins.

### arc-centric routing

all trades flow through arc, increasing volume and capturing fees in arc. this demand- and fee-driven mechanism effectively lowers arc's circulating supply over time, directly benefiting existing arc token holders.

### sniper prevention & liquidity control

by leveraging meteora's configurable bonding curve, arc forge can impose steeper price impacts on first-block snipers, curbing exploitative behaviors. after the bonding curve, project teams can reposition liquidity to foster sustainable, long-term trading.

### expanded reach via jupiter & trading bots

tokens launched on arc forge are instantly recognized by jupiter's routing system, making them tradable through popular bots like bonkbot, trojan, photon, and bullx. this ensures that new tokens gain broad exposure across existing trading tools and user bases — right from day one.

### selective launch partners (initially)

to kick things off, only vetted, high-quality projects from the arc handshake pipeline will initially be featured, ensuring a curated environment of meaningful innovation and consistent value for the greater arc community. we prioritize quality over quantity simply because we believe the "number" of launches does not determine meaningful agent applications.

### customizable liquidity strategies

projects can choose from multiple dlmm shapes — spot, curve, bid-ask — to precisely manage liquidity across different price ranges and market conditions, thus matching their desired growth trajectory or volatility tolerance.
