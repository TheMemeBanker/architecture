## about hyperbolic

an open-access decentralized AI ecosystem integrating gpu compute, verifiable inference, and developer tools:

- **gpu marketplace** — cuts costs by up to 75%, with instant access to h100, a100, 3090 and more for training and inference at scale
- **verifiable inference** — processes over 1 billion tokens daily; supports llama, qwen, stable diffusion, and tts, outputs optimized in bf16
- **proof of sampling (posp)** — a proprietary verification protocol for secure, reliable, cost-effective AI outputs; outperforms alternatives like zkml with minimal overhead
- **proven success** — powers 55,000+ developers and partners including hugging face, stanford, uc berkeley, virtuals protocol, elizaos, zerebro, and nous research
